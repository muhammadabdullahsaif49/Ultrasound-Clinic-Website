Cardiac (Echo)

Echocardiography to assess heart structure and function, reviewed by our cardiology consultant.

Overview

Echocardiography (echo) uses ultrasound to visualize the heart in motion, assessing chamber size, valve function, and pumping strength.

What's included

  • 2D Echocardiogram — Standard structural and functional heart assessment.
  • Doppler Echo — Measures blood flow across heart valves and chambers.
  • Stress Echo (Referral Basis) — Assesses heart function under physical stress.

How to prepare

No special preparation required. Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing.
