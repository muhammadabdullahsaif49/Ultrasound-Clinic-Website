Pediatric Ultrasound

Gentle, child-friendly scans for infants and children, from hip checks to abdominal pain.

Overview

Children need a calmer approach to imaging. Our pediatric scans are performed by staff experienced in working with infants and young children, with shorter wait times and a parent present throughout.

What's included

  • Infant Hip Ultrasound — Screens for developmental hip dysplasia in newborns.
  • Pediatric Abdominal Scan — Investigates abdominal pain, vomiting, or feeding issues.
  • Pediatric Renal Ultrasound — Checks kidney structure following urinary infections.

How to prepare

Bring a comfort item or toy. Feeding schedules may need adjusting — we'll advise when you book.
