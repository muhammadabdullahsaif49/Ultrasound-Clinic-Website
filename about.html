Diagnostic imaging in Rahim Yar Khan, built around the patient

Iqbal Ultrasound, located inside National Diagnostic Complex on Sheikh Zaid Hospital Road, was built on a single mission: give patients scans they can trust and reports they can actually understand. We offer all types of ultrasound and see patients throughout the week.

Our Story

Why we started

Iqbal Ultrasound was built to give patients in Rahim Yar Khan scans they can trust and reports they can actually understand, under the direction of Dr. Mudassar Iqbal, with every scan reviewed by a qualified consultant radiologist.

That principle still shapes how we hire, how we schedule appointments, and how we write our reports — in plain language, not just clinical shorthand.
